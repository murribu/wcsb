## Superintendent Search

### Work Session

At the work session, the board heard presentations from three entities who were bidding to be hired to guide the Williamson County School Board through the process of finding a new superintendent. The three entities are [[HYA|https://hyasearch.com/]], [[Zeal Education Group|https://www.zeal-ed.com/]], and [[TSBA|https://tsba.net/services/superintendent-searches/]].

The pricing schedules were not straightforward, but here are rough estimates. HYA would charge around $75,000 with add-ons available. Zeal would charge around $50,000 with add-ons available. TSBA would charge $15,000 for their most robust offering.

HYA and Zeal are both private companies and TSBA is a public entity.

TSBA is the Tennessee School Board Association. Our board pays regular dues to be in this association. Their purpose is to assist school boards in effectively governing school districts. They provide resources for many school board needs, and their superintendent search assistance is a paid offering on top of their regular services.

One wrinkle to this discussion is a new law [[HB2616|https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default?BillNumber=HB2616&ga=114]]. It basically says that a school board who is in the process of hiring a superintendent can interview candidates in private meetings. Before this law, all interviews had to be open to the public. Whichever firm is hired will need to be well-versed in this change, as Williamson County will likely be one of the first districts to operate under this new law.

### Voting Meeting

At the voting meeting, [[people.board.Randy Allen]] made a motion to hire Zeal and that kicked off the conversation.

[[people.board.Jennifer Parker Konyn]] said that she would not like to hire either of the private firms. She asked how these entities came to be the ones that we were considering and if any board member had any conflicts of interest.

Dr. Reeves explained that Zeal proactively reached out to former board member, [[people.pol.Jay Galbreath]], after hearing that [[people.o.Jason Golden]] was resigning as superintendent. Mr. Galbreath knew he wasn't going to be on the board that would make the decision, so he passed the conversation over to Dr. Reeves and [[people.admin.Rachel Farmer]]. Dr. Reeves then followed up with Zeal and in their conversation, she asked them to provide the name of a competitor of theirs. They named HYA. So that's how HYA came to the table.

Many of the other board members took their turn to say that they did not have a conflict of interest. They also took the opportunity to express why they supported their chosen option.

HYA did not seem to have any support from any board member.

[[people.board.Dennis Driggers]] repeatedly made the case for hiring TSBA. He said that they are the least expensive, we already pay them for being in their association, they know Tennessee well, they know Williamson County well. He also highlighted that Zeal is from Delaware and has never had a Tennessee client.

Ms. Anderson made the case for Zeal, saying that they have former superintendents in their employ to help the search. Also, they offer to facilitate public meetings to get input from the community. A few other board members bolstered her position in support of Zeal.

[[people.board.Jennifer Parker Konyn]] and [[people.board.Melissa Wyatt]] both expressed a willingness to either hire TSBA or eschew the search process and hire [[people.admin.Leigh Webb]]. Ms. Konyn also mentioned that both associates from Zeal who had been assigned to the WCS search team had problems in their past. She said that one was sued by their state for not correctly implementing the curriculum and the other was given a vote of no confidence.

Many of the board members mentioned Dr. Webb and how they are sure that she would shine in the interview process. And if the board decides that she's the best candidate, then the process would add confidence that they found the right person for the job.

In the end, the board voted 8-4 to hire Zeal, with Driggers, Padgett, Konyn, and Wyatt voting no.
