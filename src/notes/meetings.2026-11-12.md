The Williamson County School Board held its November work session on Thursday, the 12th. The voting meeting is Monday, the 16th, at 6:30pm.

They used a new table arrangement in the room which made it difficult to see who was absent from the camera view. So I'm not including the roll call like usual.

[Work Session Agenda](https://meeting.boeconnect.net/Public/Agenda/566?meeting=773849)

[Work Session Live Stream - 4:35:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5aV_gmTtdA)

The work session included an update on the superintendent search and two years of school calendars.

## Superintendent Search

The ThoughtExchange survey that was scheduled to close Friday, Nov 13 has been extended. Jeff Melendez of ZEAL Education Group told the board that it will remain open until **Sunday, Nov 15 at midnight**. So far, about 4,400 people have opened the survey, and about 3,400 have contributed thoughts. If you haven't filled it out yet, you have the weekend. You can [click here](https://my.thoughtexchange.com/scroll/901321891) to take the survey. It takes about 2 or 3 minutes to complete.

ZEAL spent the week in Williamson County and held 48 stakeholder meetings with students, teachers, principals, PTO presidents, the Chamber of Commerce, and others. They said that the feedback has been very consistent so far. People praised the district's professional learning communities, and everyone who talked about challenges also said they love the district.

Next, ZEAL will write a leadership profile, a roughly 15-page document summarizing the community's feedback. The board will vote on it at a special called meeting on Monday, December 7, and the job will be posted the next day.

ZEAL also proposed moving the timeline up by about a month, with the slate of candidates presented in January instead of March. A few board members raised concerns about the holidays and about new board member training in January. The board's attorneys also pointed out that while interviews can happen in closed session under the new state law, any vote to narrow the list of candidates has to happen in a public meeting. Candidates can be identified by number instead of name. [[people.board.Claire Reeves]] said that she and staff will come back with new proposed dates.

ZEAL plans to present 8-10 candidates to the board and asked board members not to request the resumes of applicants who don't make that list, to protect their confidentiality. Dr. Padgett disagreed, saying she would like to see all of the resumes. This wasn't resolved.

## Calendars

The board will vote Monday on the details of the 2027-2028 calendar and the 2028-2029 calendar. The calendar committee came back with answers to the questions the board had asked them to consider:

- 9/11 will be a remembrance day on both calendars.
- All 10 inclement weather days will be kept. These come from the extra 30 minutes that students attend school each day.
- There will be no school for students on Election Day in 2028.
- Early release days will stay on Wednesdays. The board had asked about moving them to Fridays, but the committee recommended against it. The main reason is that teachers use those afternoons for intensive planning work, which would be hard to do at the end of the week. There were also concerns about late buses on Friday nights.

The committee also asked the board to consider moving middle and high schools from Monday late starts to Wednesday early release, to match elementary schools. That way, families with kids in both would only have one off-schedule day instead of two. This is not part of Monday's vote, but it will be studied over the next year. If you have thoughts on it, now is a good time to share them.

## Other topics

- **2nd grade TCAP.** The district decided not to participate in the optional 2nd grade TCAP this year, citing concerns about whether it's developmentally appropriate and the instruction time it takes.
- **Superintendent evaluation.** The evaluation tool for Dr. Webb was pulled from the agenda. It will be revised with updated dates and brought back in January.
- **Brentwood middle school.** The County Commission approved the intent to fund for a new middle school on Split Log Road. Work will start with site prep.
