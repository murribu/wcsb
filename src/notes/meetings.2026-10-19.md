## Superintendent Search

There is a new TN law (Public Chapter 1048 (2026), originally [HB 2616](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default?BillNumber=HB2616&ga=114)) that says that local governing bodies, including school boards, may interview candidates for director-level positions, such as superintendent, in a closed session. That means that the board doesn’t have to give public notice of these interviews, and the public cannot attend. Also, applicants' names can be kept confidential. The Williamson County School Board unanimously voted in this October meeting to invoke this new law to conduct their interviews behind closed doors.

The thinking here is that this will allow a wider and better pool of candidates to apply for the job. Presumably, we would want current superintendents to apply for the job. But if they were worried about damaging their relationship with their current employer, they may be hesitant to apply. This way, their current employer would not be able to know unless that candidate was chosen for the position.

However, this means that the public has limited opportunities to speak into this important decision. ## Stephens Valley

The board unanimously voted to rezone the Stephens Valley neighborhood from the Westwood Elementary -> Fairview High zone into the Grassland Elementary -> Franklin High zone. This change makes sense for a few reasons. First, it will save the district money by not sending buses from Fairview to Stephens Valley. Second, many Stephens Valley families already attend Grassland and Franklin through open zoning, but the district doesn’t bus out-of-zone students. Rezoning means buses will serve the neighborhood, which could ease traffic on Sneed Road and Hillsboro Road. Third, Grassland and Franklin currently have capacity to take on this neighborhood and Fairview is growing, so it also makes sense from a capacity standpoint.

This rezoning takes effect with the 2027-2028 school year. The one current Fairview student from the neighborhood can choose to stay there.
