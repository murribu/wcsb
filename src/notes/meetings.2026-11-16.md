The Williamson County School Board held its November voting meeting on Monday, the 16th. All board members were present. No one signed up for public comment.

[Voting Meeting Agenda](https://meeting.boeconnect.net/Public/Agenda/566?meeting=778200)

[Voting Meeting Live Stream](https://www.youtube.com/live/E0FqyV3LVMo) - 1:01:12

[Voting Meeting Live Threads](https://www.threads.com/share/QjHSeVUKY)

There were two main items on the agenda. First, the board approved the next two school calendars. Second, after a long discussion about Fairview Elementary, the board pushed the five-year capital plan to a special called meeting on December 7.

## Motions Out Loud

[[people.board.Claire Reeves]] started a new practice as the board chair at this meeting. Whoever makes a motion or second has to do so verbally. Before this meeting, a board member would simply push a button to indicate their motion or second. It did not have a big impact on the meeting, but it was a noticeable difference.

## Calendars

The board unanimously approved the 2027-2028 calendar and the outline of the 2028-2029 calendar. For 2028-2029, they only approved the "bones": the first day, the last day, and the breaks. The details will come next year.

One change in 2028-2029 is that teachers will come back a day early. That extra day is for training on the new student information system that will replace Skyward.

[[people.board.Kimberly Calcote]] asked about Thursday's discussion of having the same early release days across the district. [[people.admin.Leigh Webb]] said the calendar committee will look at that for 2028-2029. If you have opinions about early release days and/or late start days, now would be a good time to [email the board](mailto:tony.bostic@wcs.edu,randy.allen@wcs.edu,dennis.driggers@wcs.edu,margie.padgett@wcs.edu,kimberly.calcote@wcs.edu,claire.reeves@wcs.edu,tonja.hibma@wcs.edu,jennifer.konyn@wcs.edu,melissa.wyatt@wcs.edu,traci.anderson@wcs.edu,donna.clements@wcs.edu,jessica.lucyshyn@wcs.edu) with those thoughts.

## Five-Year Capital Plan

This is where the meeting got interesting.

Ms. Calcote made a motion to approve the five-year capital plan as presented, and [[people.board.Tonja Hibma]] seconded. During the discussion, [[people.board.Tony Bostic]] made a motion to amend the plan to move the new Fairview Elementary up a year, and [[people.board.Margie Padgett]] seconded.

The new Fairview Elementary is a $55 million project. In the draft plan, the first $2.5 million comes in 2027-2028 and $30 million comes the year after. Mr. Bostic's amendment would move each of those up one year. The total for the plan would not change.

Mr. Bostic said that Fairview Elementary was built in 1962, is the oldest school in the district, and keeps getting pushed back. "It's about disparity and about a building that is falling apart," he said. He invited board members and county commissioners to come tour the Fairview schools.

[[people.admin.Rachel Farmer]] explained why it had been pushed back. Fairview Elementary has 407 students in a building with room for 660, and enrollment there is flat. Education impact fees can only be spent in response to growth, so they can't be used for this school right now. It would have to be paid for some other way, and that decision is up to the County Commission.

[[people.board.Donna Clements]] and [[people.board.Jennifer Parker Konyn]] both spoke in favor of the idea. [[people.board.Randy Allen]] said he couldn't tell what the numbers would look like, and suggested waiting so the board could compare two versions of the plan side by side.

Ms. Hibma then made a motion to amend the motion, pushing the capital plan to the December 7 special called meeting. Ms. Calcote seconded, and that passed 11-1, with Mr. Bostic voting no.

Then things got confusing. They never voted on Mr. Bostic's original amendment, but Ms. Hibma's had now passed. Mr. Bostic was not clear about what they had just voted on and asked if he could go back and change his "no" to a "yes." Regardless, the board then voted 12-0 on the motion as amended, which officially moved the capital plan to December 7.

## Superintendent Search

Dr. Reeves announced that ZEAL will present the leadership profile at the December 7 special called meeting, and the board will vote on it then. Applications for superintendent will open December 8 and close January 4.

## Call to Action

Usually, the board does not meet in December, but there will be a special called meeting to vote on the Superintendent Profile from ZEAL on Monday, December 7.
